# Komofuu 0.14.8

- Opening Komofuu again on Windows now shows the existing companion instead of
  starting another app instance and another set of WebViews. Selected-window OCR
  keeps its separate, short-lived capture helper.
- Update the application EULA to version 1.1 and synchronize the publisher,
  privacy and legal information supplied with the app and on the public site.
- Keep publisher identification in the dedicated disclosure and bundled legal
  information, reducing repetition of personal details in ordinary project docs.
- Preserve existing saved words, notes, dictionaries, preferences and updater
  identity. Earlier releases retain their own terms and matching legal files;
  earlier MIT grants and independent third-party rights remain unaffected.

Read the EULA and privacy notice supplied with this version. Updater signatures
verify the package against the existing update key; they are separate from
Windows publisher signing.
