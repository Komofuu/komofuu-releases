Privacy questions and requests

Email the project contact above for privacy questions or requests concerning information you have shared with Komofuu. Describe your request and include only the information needed to explain it. This address is supplied by the publisher; requests are handled as required by applicable law. No additional voluntary response-time guarantee is offered.

App data stored only on your device is managed through the controls described below; the project cannot remotely access or delete it. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting route in SECURITY.md, or the project email as a fallback.

Support correspondence and reports

For messages and reports sent to the project, the publisher identified in the disclosure linked above acts as controller. This can include your email address or GitHub username, message, app/device details and attachments you choose to provide. It does not give the publisher access to app data kept only on your device.

The purpose is to answer requests, investigate bugs and security reports, and protect the project and users. The legal basis is legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining security (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); responding to statutory requests also relies on legal obligations where applicable (Article 6(1)(c)). Providing an ordinary support message is optional; without enough information the project may be unable to investigate it. There is no automated decision-making or profiling using support correspondence.

Email passes through Google, which hosts the project Gmail inbox. GitHub handles issues and private vulnerability reports; public issues and attachments are visible to others. Providers can process information internationally. See Google's privacy policy and transfer frameworks (https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks) and GitHub's privacy statement linked below for their arrangements. No exclusive EU storage or separate signed processor agreement is claimed.

Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, follow-up, security investigation and any applicable legal obligations or claims. Retention depends on the issue's status, sensitivity and continuing need; unnecessary private attachments should be removed rather than retained as general project history.

Contact the privacy email above to exercise applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection. These rights have legal conditions and exceptions. You may complain to Romania's ANSPDCP (https://www.dataprotection.ro/) or another competent EU supervisory authority.

Definitions, clipboard and pronunciation

Definitions use only dictionaries you download and import. There is no online dictionary fallback or online recording service. No dictionary data is included with the installer. Imported Yomitan ZIPs and EDICT2 files are indexed locally in SQLite, with their titles, revisions, attribution, selections and order. Import does not contact dictionary URLs or upload the file. Download-page buttons open predefined project pages in your default browser only when pressed. Those sites receive normal browser/connection information; downloads remain your choice.

Automatic clipboard lookup is on by default unless you previously disabled it. While enabled, Komofuu reads the current clipboard after a Windows change notification, filters for Japanese text up to 200 characters and performs local dictionary lookup. It keeps no clipboard history. Manual lookup reads the clipboard when requested. Saving a word can retain its sentence. Native-text lookup reads accessible text under the pointer on request and attempts to skip password controls. No synthetic Copy, keyboard recording or game injection is used.

Listen and Read aloud use an installed Japanese Windows speech voice. Komofuu does not download recordings, implement a cloud speech API or record synthesized speech into Anki. Windows voices and WebView2 are governed by their own platform settings and terms. Old recording URLs/preferences are not used. Existing saved vocabulary and Anki media are not deleted; their original data rights still apply.

Local storage, OCR and companion behavior

The installer records the accepted application EULA and bundled-component terms revision locally in the current Windows user’s uninstall registry entry. This record is not uploaded; normal uninstall removes that entry. New or changed terms require another review.

Configured global mouse shortcuts observe only mouse buttons and the current Ctrl/Alt/Shift modifier state. They do not record typed text, keep an input history or upload input events. Keyboard shortcuts use Windows shortcut registration.

No Komofuu account, advertising, analytics, telemetry or developer-operated upload server is implemented. Preferences, saved words, dictionary indexes and WebView notes and profile data are stored in this Windows account's application profile. They are not an encrypted vault. Someone with access to that account may read them.

Notes keep their text, title, transparency, pin state and window position in a bounded local notes.json file in the app-data folder. They are not uploaded or sent to Anki, OCR or other services. Closing a note keeps it in the Notes list; Delete note removes it from active storage. Updates preserve notes and the uninstaller’s app-data deletion includes them. Deletion is not secure erasure.

PaddleOCR and optional ScreenAI process requested captures locally. Scan status and controls indicate readiness; the optional OCR border is a visual guide. Scanning normally starts only on your shortcut, Scan now, or releasing an explicitly drawn crop. Continuous local scanning is optional, off by default and not recommended for ordinary use. Local OCR/native-text temporary jobs use a leased process-specific app-data session. Normal exit cleans the session; startup removes abandoned sessions without deleting another active process's files. Crashes, locked files or deletion errors can delay cleanup. Worker diagnostics retain only a 16 KiB tail in memory. No secure-erasure or forensic-removal guarantee is made.

ScreenAI is an optional Google component. Choosing Set up ScreenAI downloads a pinned component from dl.google.com (with permitted Google CDN redirects), verifies it and runs a synthetic Japanese recognition test. This download exposes ordinary connection information such as your IP address to the download provider, but does not capture or upload your screen. Choosing the engine or Check engine does not download it. Its models and complete third-party notices stay in local Komofuu app data. Recognition runs in the isolated OCR worker with no image upload; models are released after the worker's existing idle timeout, OCR stop, or engine switch. App-data deletion during uninstall includes this optional component. Its own third-party licences apply.

The latest original OCR scan and bounded dictionary results are cached in memory. Stopping OCR clears the original-scan cache. Native Lens encodes its image in memory without starting the Python OCR worker. Application-window capture uses a short-lived helper and an OS-managed temporary image file, removed when its handles close. This applies to all OCR engines. Window titles and target IDs stay local and are not persisted as capture presets or sent as Lens metadata. The chosen window is checked for each scan; a closed, minimized, protected or unsupported window produces an error without falling back to a desktop image. Cursor position, Windows inactivity, companion position and island state support local animation. Optional music reactions read scalar Windows output levels; they use no microphone, recording or audio upload and pause in island/Gaming mode.

Optional Google Lens

Only choosing Lens and granting its separate upload permission allows requested scans to send the captured area to Google. Full-screen scans include everything visible on that monitor; choose a smaller area to share less. Lens scans are manual: no background uploads, silent retries or online fallback from PaddleOCR. No Google account/browser cookies are supplied by this adapter.

This is an unofficial integration. It can stop working. Upload consent is a user control, not proof of Google's endorsement or service authorization. Google receives the capture and normal connection information including IP address. Komofuu cannot promise Google-side retention, deletion or regional processing. Data goes directly to Google without a Komofuu proxy.

Provider information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/terms . No processor agreement or hosting guarantee is claimed here.

Anki and updates

Anki integration is enabled explicitly. Connecting and mining communicate only with AnkiConnect at http://127.0.0.1:8765 on your computer. An optional API key is kept in native memory for the session, not stored in preferences. Card fields can contain the word, reading, meaning, source sentence, dictionary metadata and tags. OCR screenshots are independently optional, cropped from the original scan rather than a new capture, and stored in Anki. New cards receive no recording attachments; the legacy Audio field/marker remains empty for note-type compatibility.

Anki may upload cards/images through its own synchronization settings. Disabling integration or uninstalling Komofuu does not delete Anki cards or media. Mining failures can leave unused media when an outcome is uncertain; Komofuu does not run a collection-wide media deletion. See Anki's Tools → Check Media.

Configured release builds check GitHub for updates on request. Startup checks are optional and off by default; download and installation each need your action. GitHub receives normal request/IP information, not dictionary queries, clipboard text, saved words or screenshots. Update signatures authenticate packages; they do not certify the absence of bugs. Builds without a configured feed/key make no update requests. GitHub hosting policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Website, controls and deletion

The supplied website has no app analytics, advertisements, external fonts or cookies set by its code. It requests public GitHub release metadata. Hosting, downloads and project links still involve the respective hosts' normal logging. Public GitHub issues and their attachments are visible to others. Do not post private captures, secrets, personal clipboard contents or vulnerability details.

Disable automatic clipboard lookup to stop automatic clipboard reads. Leave Lens permission off and use PaddleOCR to keep OCR local. Delete saved entries, or export a backup under Saved words. Enable, disable, reorder and remove dictionaries under Settings → Dictionaries. To update one, remove its old revision and import a new download. Removal preserves the original ZIP and saved words; SQLite compaction reclaims space when possible and reports incomplete compaction.

In an installed release, the confirmed Uninstall and delete app data action opens the Windows uninstaller with profile deletion selected. Normal Windows uninstall offers a data-removal choice. Updates keep app data. Exports, downloaded ZIPs and installers, Anki cards/media, other Windows accounts' data and shared Windows components are outside Komofuu's full-data removal. Locked files can require later cleanup and are reported. This is deletion, not secure erasure. A cancelled Windows uninstall keeps the app/data. Verify this behavior on the final installer.

Local recovery and diagnostics

Komofuu keeps up to five local recovery snapshots each for saved words/selected preferences and notes. Each snapshot is limited to 8 MiB. Automatic snapshots are paced to no more than one per category per 30 minutes; manual profile backups and protection before note deletion/recovery can create an additional snapshot. Recovery files contain personal study data and are not encrypted by Komofuu. They remain in this Windows profile's app-data folder, with no upload, and are removed when app data is deleted during uninstall. Manual exports remain separate. If damaged notes are repaired, the unreadable original is preserved as notes-before-recovery.json (up to 4 MiB). Note recovery adds closed copies rather than overwriting healthy current notes.

Diagnostics are generated only when requested in Advanced settings. They contain app/component versions, selected engine/animation flags, permission flags and counts/sizes and recent numeric lookup timings/cache flags. The OCR overlay keeps at most 24 samples each for foreground resolution, background resolution and popup construction in memory. Timings are not saved to application storage and disappear when the overlay restarts. They exclude vocabulary/definitions, note contents, screenshots, clipboard text, dictionary titles, usernames, file paths and credentials. An export is downloaded locally; Komofuu does not send it to support automatically.

Setup guide and last cropped area

Komofuu remembers one recent cropped rectangle and its screen arrangement/scaling in local settings. This stores pixel coordinates and display dimensions, not a screenshot, recognized text or application-window title. After restarting, reuse is explicit and checks the screen layout; nothing is captured automatically. Choosing a new area replaces the remembered rectangle. These local preferences are removed when Komofuu app data is deleted during uninstall.

The quick-start guide remembers whether it was dismissed/opened and whether a first successful screen lookup was completed. That milestone is a boolean, not a record of the word or definition. Opening the guide checks local dictionary and installed-component metadata; it does not capture, initialize an OCR model, download components or grant Google Lens upload permission.

Reopening Komofuu on Windows

A normal second launch of Komofuu 0.14.8 exits and asks the already-running app to show its companion. It does not start another set of app windows or create another profile. This local single-instance notification does not upload launch arguments or application data. The short-lived selected-window capture helper remains a separate process and follows the local capture behavior above.
